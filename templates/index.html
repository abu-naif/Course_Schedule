How Course Combinator Works

        

        
      


      

        

          
1. Conflict-Free Guarantee

          
The combinator algorithm checks every single possible pairing of sections across your selected courses to ensure that no theory classes, no labs, and no final exams overlap with one another.

        


        

          
2. Mandatory Section Constraints

          
If you specifically want a particular section (e.g. section 01 for CSE110), you can type CSE110:01 in the mandatory section box. Combinations without this section are automatically excluded.

        


        

          
3. Faculty Preferences & Avoidance

          
Enter faculty initials (e.g. AIB, DZK). The engine prioritizes schedules with your preferred instructors. If you check Mandatory, any section not matching preferred faculty is dropped.

        


        

          
4. Skip Class Times

          
Avoid early morning 8:00 AM slots or late evening slots with one click.

        


        

          
5. Export & Sharing

          
Export conflict-free schedules to PDF or CSV reports, copy formatted text for Discord, or print directly.
